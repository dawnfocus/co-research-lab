# Review literature

1. Define the research question, coverage window, source types, and inclusion/exclusion criteria.
2. Search appropriate primary scholarly sources. Preserve exact queries when coverage or novelty matters.
3. Verify metadata and claims against the original paper or authoritative record; do not cite search snippets as evidence.
4. Record relevant sources in `research/literature/INDEX.md` with stable links, relevance, and the exact evidence used.
5. Distinguish authors' claims, reported results, and this project's interpretation.
6. Update affected findings, experiment plans, paper claims, and known unknowns. Note unresolved access or coverage gaps.

Do not commit unauthorized full-text papers. Store only permitted metadata, notes, quotations within applicable limits, and links.
